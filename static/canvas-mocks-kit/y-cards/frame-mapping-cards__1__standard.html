Program Levels and Requirements

Teams of all levels are coached by YMCA volunteers. Registration includes a jersey and socks. Shin guards are required for all age groups, and cleats are recommended for ages 9 and up.

Ages 3-4 Coed

This is an introduction to the sport that focuses on social skills, teamwork and fundamentals. This level will have six practices and games.

Ages 5-6 Coed

At this level, we nurture skill development, teamwork, positioning and fun. Teams play seven games throughout the season and use a size 3 ball.

Ages 7-8 Boys/7–8 Girls

Well-rounded instruction continues with an introduction to strategy added in. Teams play seven games and may travel to play other Ys in the area. A size 4 ball is used.
